💳 Add Credits to Your Workspace

Pay with USDC on Base to add credits to your ProceedGate workspace. Credits are used for governor checks.

📋 How payment works

  1. Enter your workspace ID and API key — you got these when you created the workspace
  2. Create a quote — this generates a payment request with amount and recipient address
  3. Pay with your wallet — send USDC on Base network (MetaMask, Coinbase Wallet, etc.)
  4. Redeem the transaction — paste the tx hash to credit your account instantly
